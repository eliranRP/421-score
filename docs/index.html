421 score

Café paper feuille. Support.

EN FR

421 score is a French café feuille — a paper scoresheet for 421. You write charges with large +/− steppers. It does not roll dice or play the game.

Local on the phone only: no account, no tracking, no network. Last player names stay on the device.

Help

  • 2–6 players, optional names
  • House-rule markers: 421, nénette, macque
  • Next round, undo, reset round, reset table

Questions or a problem: open a GitHub issue.
