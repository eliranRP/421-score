This app is a local café scoresheet for 421. It keeps the current score on your phone. It does not require an account.

What we collect

Nothing.

The app does not create an account, does not sign you in, and does not send your score or device data to us or to anyone else. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporter, and no third-party tracker.

Permissions

The app does not use camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, or tracking permission. v1 does not need a network connection.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all local match data. There is no cloud copy to delete.

Contact

GitHub issues.

Changes

We will post updates here if the app starts collecting data. v1 does not.
